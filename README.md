# Paradux

Paradux is a two-player board game. Play together on one device or invite a friend to play online.

## How to play

**Get four or more marbles of your color in a straight line to win.**

Black goes first.

On your turn, select two adjacent marbles of different colors (⚫️⚪️).

- **Slide** both marbles one space in the same direction without rotating the pair. Choose a highlighted destination.
- **Switch** the two marbles to swap their positions (⚫️⚪️ → ⚪️⚫️).

If one move forms a winning line for both colors, play continues.

Have fun!
